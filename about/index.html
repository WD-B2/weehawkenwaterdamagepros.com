About Weehawken Water Damage Pros

.

A local line for a stressful moment. Here is how we work, what we do and what we leave to others.

Call (844) 950-6900Dispatch line open 24/7
.

Who we are

Weehawken Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service based at 1200 Harbor Boulevard in Weehawken, New Jersey. We answer a 24/7 line, send crews for water extraction and drying and explain scope and price before work starts. We are licensed and insured.

We keep our claims modest. This site does not list awards, years in business or job counts because we would rather you judge us by what we explain and what we put in writing. Ask us anything on the first call, and compare our answers with anyone else you phone.

What we do

Our work covers the full range of residential and commercial water losses, from a leaking appliance to a flooded basement. The services page lists all twenty services. The most common calls involve water extraction, burst pipes, sewage backup and structural drying.

We also handle mold remediation when moisture has lingered. We do not diagnose health conditions and we do not give medical advice. If you have health questions, please talk with a doctor.

How a job runs

  1. Call and triage

    You tell us what happened, where the water is and whether it is still running. We ask about electrical hazards and building access.

  2. Arrival and assessment

    The crew finds the source, checks what is wet and takes moisture readings.

  3. Scope and price

    We explain what will be done and what it costs before work starts. Changes are discussed before they happen.

  4. Extraction and drying

    Standing water is removed and air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure. Readings are logged.

  5. Verification and records

    We check readings before removing equipment and supply photos and logs for your records.

Standards we follow

The industry publishes standards for water damage restoration, including ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold remediation. We refer to them as published references. We do not claim a certification on this site, and we will tell you plainly which parts of a job fall outside our work.

Insurance questions belong with your insurer. We can provide photos, moisture readings and a written scope for your claim, but coverage decisions are theirs.

Why Weehawken

Weehawken is a township on the Palisades, home to the New Jersey side of the Lincoln Tunnel and a stretch of Hudson River waterfront. Alexander Hamilton and Aaron Burr dueled here in 1804. For us, the geography matters more than the history: steep streets, older multi-unit buildings and a busy waterfront mean access and building rules shape almost every job.

We serve Fairview, Jersey City and Ridgefield as well. The service area page lists the other towns we reach.

What we ask of customers

We ask three things. Tell us the truth about what happened, including anything you already tried. Tell us who else needs to be involved, such as a landlord, a building manager or an insurer. And tell us what you need, whether that is a quick dry-out, a scope for a claim or an answer about whether a problem needs a crew at all.

In return, you get plain language, a written scope and a price you can compare. If we are not the right fit, we will say so. A short honest call is better than a long job that should have gone to a plumber, an electrician or your building engineer.

Where our work stops

Restoration is not plumbing, electrical work or roofing. We stop water, remove it and dry the structure, and we remove materials that cannot be saved. When a pipe needs replacing, a panel needs inspecting or a roof needs patching, a licensed trade does that work, and we coordinate timing so drying is not delayed.

We also do not decide insurance coverage, give legal advice or diagnose health conditions. Those belong to your insurer, your attorney and your doctor. Knowing the limits of our role keeps expectations clear and keeps you from paying for work that does not fit the problem.

What to do before we arrive

Safety steps while you wait

  • Stop the water at the nearest shutoff if you can reach it safely.
  • Stay away from wet outlets, the panel and sagging ceilings.
  • Keep children and pets out of the wet area.
  • Photograph the damage before moving anything.
  • Lift small valuables onto dry surfaces.

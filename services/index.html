Water damage is not one problem. A burst pipe, a failed water heater, a storm and a sewer backup each call for a different response, so we organize services by cause. Start with the group that fits your situation and open the page for details on how the work is done.

Our descriptions of drying and documentation follow the published industry reference, ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard for professional water damage restoration. Mold pages refer to its companion, S520. We name them as published references, not as claims about certification.

Every page explains the first steps you can take yourself, what the crew does on arrival and what to ask before approving work. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and the 24/7 line is open if you want to talk it through.

Many losses involve more than one service. A burst pipe may need water extraction, then structural drying and dehumidification, then wall repair. A storm may bring flood damage restoration and basement water damage cleanup together.

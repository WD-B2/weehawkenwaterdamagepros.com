What to have ready

When you call

  • The street address and unit number.
  • Where the water is coming from, if you know.
  • Whether the water is still running and whether you have shut it off.
  • Whether any water is near outlets, a panel or lights.
  • Your building manager or landlord contact, if you rent or own a condo.
  • Your insurer's name, if you have already reported the loss.

Before the crew arrives

Stop the water at the nearest shutoff if it is safe. Keep clear of wet electrical areas, photograph the damage and lift small valuables to dry surfaces. For more detail, see water extraction and burst pipe water damage.

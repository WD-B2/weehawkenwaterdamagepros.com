24/7 emergency water damage restoration, every day

Pipes do not wait for business hours. A supply line can fail at three in the morning and a storm can arrive on a holiday. The 24/7 line exists for those moments, and a call at night is handled the same way as a call at noon.

When you call, we ask where the water is, what it came from and whether it is still running. If it is, we help you find the shutoff. If it is near outlets or a panel, we tell you to stay clear. Same-day response is offered when available, and we say plainly if timing is tight.

For burst pipes, read burst pipe water damage. For leaks from above, see ceiling water damage repair. If sewage is involved, tell us right away so the right equipment and protective gear arrive with the crew.

What water damage repair costs depend on

We do not list prices here because no number fits every job. Cost follows the amount of water, how long it sat, which materials it reached, how hard they are to reach and whether anything must be rebuilt afterward. A wet bathroom floor and a flooded basement are very different projects.

Timing affects cost more than most people expect. Water that is removed in the first day usually leaves more of the structure dry and fewer materials to replace. Water that sits for several days soaks deeper into drywall, insulation and subfloor, and the scope grows. That is why we encourage a call even when you are not sure the situation is serious.

What we can promise is clarity. You get a scope and a price before work starts, you can ask what is included and what is not, and changes are discussed before they happen. If you are comparing companies, our water extraction page explains what a good scope contains.

Insurance and water damage claims

Coverage depends on your policy and on how the water got in. We supply photos, moisture readings and a written scope. Your insurer decides what is covered. Read about flood damage restoration and sewage backup cleanup if those apply.

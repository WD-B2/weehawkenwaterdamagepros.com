A wet basement is an emergency because water below grade sits against walls, stored belongings and often the home's mechanical systems. It may be clean seepage, rainwater, or contaminated backup, and you cannot tell by looking. The 24/7 line is answered at night and during storms, and the first rule is to stay out if power or gas equipment is near the water.

Weehawken basements are a common concern, and the neighborhood page for The Shades covers lower-level water in the downtown area.

When basement water is an emergency

Immediate steps

  • Do not enter if water covers outlets, the panel, a furnace or a water heater. Call the utility or 911 if you suspect electrical danger.
  • Stop using water in the building if you think it is a sewer backup.
  • Move belongings off the floor from the stairs if you can do so safely.
  • Photograph the waterline and damaged items before moving them.
  • Call the line and describe the depth, the color of the water and whether it is still rising.

How basement cleanup works

  1. Pump and extract

    Pumps remove standing water, and wet vacuums clear the rest.

  2. Remove what cannot dry

    Carpet, pad, damaged drywall and insulation come out, especially if the water was contaminated.

  3. Clean and dry

    Hard surfaces are cleaned and sanitized as needed. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry walls, framing and concrete.

  4. Verify and rebuild

    Readings confirm dryness. Finished spaces are rebuilt after.

Honest limit: restoration is not waterproofing or foundation repair. If water keeps returning, a contractor who handles drainage or waterproofing should look at the cause. We remove the water and dry the space so the next step can happen.

Finished basements and contents

Finished basements raise the stakes because drywall, flooring, furniture and electronics all sit low. Contents are sorted by what can be cleaned and what cannot. Documents and photographs can sometimes be dried by a specialist if you keep them cold and call quickly.

Sump pumps, power and what to check

If your basement has a sump pump, check that it is running and the discharge line is clear of ice or debris before the next storm. A pump that depends on household power fails in an outage, so a battery backup is a common precaution that a plumber can install. None of this replaces drying after water has entered.

Before anyone goes downstairs after a flood, check whether furnaces, water heaters and panels are in the wet area. If they are, the utility or an electrician should confirm they are safe before power is restored.

Questions people ask

Do you handle basement flooding emergencies at night or on weekends in Weehawken?

Yes. The line is open 24 hours a day, including storms and holidays. Crews are sent the same day when available. Stay out if water is near electrical equipment and take photos.

Is basement water always contaminated?

Not always, but you cannot tell by looking. Water from a plumbing leak starts clean, while storm and drain water is treated as contaminated.

Can you stop my basement from flooding again?

No. Restoration removes water and dries the space. Waterproofing, drainage and sump systems are a different trade.

Should I run a dehumidifier myself?

A small unit helps with humidity after water is removed, but it cannot dry wet walls and concrete quickly. Get readings first.

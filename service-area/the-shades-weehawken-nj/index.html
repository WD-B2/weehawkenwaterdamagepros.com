Water damage restoration in The Shades, Weehawken

The Shades is the downtown part of Weehawken, below the cliffs. Public real estate listings show single-family, multi-family and condo properties, so a water problem can involve one household or several.

For a property in this part of town, the work starts the same way as anywhere in Weehawken. Find the source, stop it, remove the water and dry the structure. The details change with the building, and lower levels get extra attention.

Lower levels, basements and drains

Water that enters from above runs downhill inside a building, so ground floors and basements often take the worst of it. Basements can also take on water from outside during heavy rain. A township letter from December 2023 said the North Hudson Sewerage Authority project aimed to fix old infrastructure that frequently floods streets and basements in intense storms.

That statement covers the township and not The Shades alone.

Basement water

Finished basements hold carpet, drywall and stored items that soak up water fast. The basement water damage cleanup page explains the process.

Drain and sewer backups

Water from a backed up drain can carry contamination. Do not wade in it, and read about sewage backup cleanup.

Heavy rain and flooding

Water from outside can enter through doors, windows and floor drains. See flood damage restoration for scope and safety.

What to do while you wait

  1. Turn off power to wet areas

    If the basement has standing water near outlets, do not enter. Leave the breaker alone unless the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.

  2. Do not wade through suspected sewage

    Water from drains can contain bacteria. Keep children and pets away and wash hands after contact with anything wet.

  3. Stop what you can

    Close the main shutoff if a pipe is the cause. If the water is coming from outside, there is nothing to shut off, so move to documenting.

  4. Photograph the waterline

    Take photos of the depth marks on walls and of damaged belongings. Add a few from the doorway.

  5. Keep wet materials until documented

    Do not discard carpet, boxes or drywall before taking pictures and noting what they were.

Extraction and drying in a lower level

Basement jobs often begin with pumping, followed by removal of any porous material that cannot be dried in place. Concrete and masonry hold moisture for a long time, so drying is checked with meters before anything is rebuilt, following ANSI/IICRC S500.

  1. Pump and extract

    Standing water is removed with pumps and extractors. Water extraction covers the equipment.

  2. Remove what cannot be saved

    Soaked carpet pad, insulation and some drywall come out, because they stay wet and can grow mold.

  3. Dry and dehumidify

    Dehumidifiers and air movers run until readings return to normal. See structural drying and dehumidification.

  4. Check for mold

    If dampness lingers, mold remediation may be needed before rebuilding.

Insurance documentation and flood zone status

Questions people ask

Where is The Shades in Weehawken?

It is the downtown area below the cliffs. Boundaries are informal, so give your street address when you call and we will confirm it is within the service area.

What should I do if my basement floods?

Stay out if water is near outlets or the panel, photograph the waterline and call. If a pipe is the source, close the main shutoff. Do not run household vacuums on standing water.

Is water from a backed up drain dangerous?

It can be. Water from drains and sewers may carry bacteria, so avoid contact, keep children and pets away and call. Cleanup uses protective gear and disinfection.

Will my insurance cover basement water?

It depends on the policy and on how the water got in. Flood, sewer backup and burst pipes are often treated differently, so ask your insurer before repairs begin.
